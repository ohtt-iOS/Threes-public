Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-09-18

In short

Threes has no account and no server of its own. Everything you record stays on your device. We do not collect personal data; the only thing that leaves your phone is anonymous usage statistics (see Network use).

Camera and Photos

Threes asks for the camera to record your clips and for permission to add to Photos so it can save your finished video. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Data on your device

Clips, thumbnails, drafts, fonts you add and your settings are stored only on your device. They are removed when you delete them in the app or delete the app.

Purchases

Threes Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details or Apple ID. Restoring a purchase also goes through Apple.

Network use

The app connects to the internet only to check whether a newer version exists, by asking Apple's App Store about the app's own identifier and your store region, and possibly by fetching a small update-notice file from our hosting. No device identifiers are sent. Threes also uses Google Analytics for Firebase to count how the app is used: which screens open, which templates and filters are chosen, whether a three is finished or a purchase is made. This sends a random app-instance ID, device model, OS version, language and approximate region to Google. It never includes your videos, photos, labels or any account — there is none. No advertising identifier is used and there is no advertising or cross-app tracking.

Sharing

When you share a video, it is handed to the app you choose, and that app's privacy policy applies.

Children

Threes is not directed at children under 13 and, as described above, collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at https://github.com/ohtt-iOS/Threes-public/issues.
